About WorldScope

WorldScope is an interactive country explorer built to help people discover the richness and diversity of nations across the globe. From flags and capitals to languages and populations, we bring the world to your fingertips.

Whether you're a student, traveler, or simply curious about the world, WorldScope provides a clean and intuitive interface to browse and learn.

  • Explore 250+ countries with detailed data
  • Filter by region, search by name
  • Save your favorite countries locally
  • Responsive design for all devices
  • Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
