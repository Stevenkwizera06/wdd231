TechHub Rwanda

Site Plan Document

01

Site Name

TechHub Rwanda

Reason for Name Choice

The name "TechHub Rwanda" was chosen because it clearly communicates the site's purpose as a central platform (hub) for Rwanda's technology ecosystem. The name is memorable, professional, and immediately conveys that this is a technology-focused resource specifically for Rwanda. It positions the site as the go-to destination for anyone interested in Rwanda's tech scene, innovation spaces, and digital transformation initiatives.

02

Site Purpose

🚀

Showcase Innovation

Highlight Rwanda's thriving technology ecosystem, including innovation hubs, tech startups, and digital transformation success stories.

🎓

Educational Resources

Provide comprehensive information about tech education programs, coding bootcamps, and skill development opportunities available in Rwanda.

🤝

Create a platform where tech enthusiasts, entrepreneurs, investors, and students can discover events, networking opportunities, and collaborative spaces.

📊

03

User Scenarios

Scenario 1

The Aspiring Tech Entrepreneur

Question: "I have a tech startup idea but don't know where to start in Rwanda. What innovation hubs and incubators are available, and what resources do they offer?"

How the site helps: The site will feature a comprehensive directory of innovation hubs with detailed information about their programs, application processes, success stories, and contact information. Users can filter by location, focus area, and available resources.

Scenario 2

The International Investor

Question: "I'm interested in investing in Rwanda's tech sector. What are the most promising startups, and what is the current state of the technology ecosystem?"

How the site helps: The site will provide data-driven insights about Rwanda's tech ecosystem, including featured startups, funding statistics, success metrics, and investment opportunities. Interactive visualizations will make complex data easily digestible.

Scenario 3

The Career-Switching Professional

Question: "I want to transition into a tech career. What training programs and bootcamps are available in Rwanda, and which companies are hiring?"

How the site helps: The site will showcase educational programs, coding bootcamps, and certification courses. It will also feature a job board with current tech opportunities and company profiles, helping users make informed career decisions.

04

Color Schema

Primary Gradient

#667eea → #764ba2

Usage: Headers, hero sections, call-to-action buttons, and accent elements. This vibrant gradient represents innovation and technology.

Dark Background

#1a1a2e

Usage: Main background color, navigation bar, and footer. Creates a modern, premium feel and excellent contrast.

Secondary Dark

#16213e

Usage: Card backgrounds, section dividers, and content containers. Provides depth and visual hierarchy.

Accent Blue

#0f3460

Usage: Hover states, borders, and interactive elements. Adds depth to the color scheme.

Highlight Red

#e94560

Usage: Important notifications, featured items, and emphasis points. Draws attention to key elements.

Light Text

#f0f0f0

Usage: Primary text color on dark backgrounds. Ensures excellent readability and accessibility.
